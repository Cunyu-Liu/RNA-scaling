# 6. Limitations

This section asks which conclusions remain under-identified after the controlled interventions and which reporting gaps constrain their interpretation. The decisive audit crosses composition baselines, external-corpus exposure, unfinished arms, training-seed coverage, epoch coverage, rRNA stratification, readout protocols, and checkpoint-level provenance. The resulting picture is that every main claim has an explicit boundary control, but none of those controls licenses extrapolation beyond the measured arms, protocols, and evidence grades.

| Limitation | Consequence for interpretation | Boundary control already applied |
|---|---|---|
| **B3: learned weight structure and composition reading are not separable.** Randinit and moment-matched controls establish that the advantage requires learned weights, but neither control reads sequence composition; classical baselines can therefore capture overlapping signal. | A positive trained-minus-control effect is not automatically a beyond-composition effect, and the family-split margin depends on baseline and protocol. | S9 crosses split and protocol with k-mer logistic regression, LightGBM, a supervised one-hot CNN, and random embeddings, while S4/S5 provide the intervention arm. The primary mean-pool contrast explicitly uses LightGBM s17 only; balanced s17 sensitivity is reported separately rather than replacing it with the former k-mer comparison. |
| **External pretraining corpora are a bidirectional black box.** Published models may contain homologues of the evaluation families, while their sequence manifests cannot be audited against the S0 partitions. | External scores can be affected in either direction by unknown exposure and are jointly confounded by architecture, corpus, and training budget; they cannot support controlled corpus attribution. | Cluster and exact-sequence isolation is asserted only for the self-trained family. The new external COV gradient and its randinit controls are labeled observational anchors; controlled corpus claims continue to use same-architecture recipe-replica and rfamcap arms instead. |
| **The rfamcap structure effect does not explain the cross-model structure gap.** The adjudicated terminal rfamcap-minus-main differences are +1.77 pp at 30M and +0.14 pp at 100M under paired-F1, whereas the external gap under discussion is approximately +0.10 F1. | Corpus enrichment alone is insufficient to account for that cross-model difference; architecture, corpus source, exposure, and readout remain confounded. | The rfamcap arm retains the architecture and provides same-seed terminal contrasts with randinit floors in place. Both family and structure effects decay with capacity, but the single-seed TS0 best-layer structure results remain descriptive and are not used to attribute the external gap. |
| **The historical rfamcap structure result is not reproducible.** Neither the 1.8B nor the 1.9B candidate checkpoint reproduced any of the 23 historical layers (0/23 exact-layer matches for each). | The historical `0.5924@L20` and its +0.34 pp contrast are withdrawn; only the new terminal paired-F1 contrasts are current. | The checkpoint adjudication directly evaluated both candidates and added the missing 30M structure arm. This resolves the former checkpoint-label gap without claiming that the unknown historical source has been identified. |
| **MARS and RNAcentral corpus arms are unfinished.** MARS has no completed terminal probes, and RNAcentral has no retrievable checkpoints in the authoritative backup; several MARS arms retain only low-budget midpoints. | The evidence does not support a three-corpus spectrum or terminal comparisons for these arms. | A run-level registry records zero probe rows, available checkpoints, and empty directories. No missing arm is treated as a negative result; the corpus claim remains restricted to the main recipe, the recipe replica, and the rfamcap orthogonal point. |
| **Training-seed coverage is uneven at 300M and 650M.** Both tiers contain only training seed s17. | Their endpoint differences, saturation, layer locations, and budget responses have no training-seed uncertainty estimate. | Single-seed entries are shown without fabricated error bars and are separated from the three-training-seed 1M–100M summaries. The unreviewed probe-seed retest is excluded from current evidence and is not independent pretraining replication. |
| **The 10M attrition is a mean-level phenomenon.** The 10M mean is below the 1M mean, but the paired change is negative in only two of three training seeds. | A universal per-seed decline or a precise seed-invariant magnitude is not supported. | The three paired training seeds are retained in the main table, the time axis locates a 0.5B-nt family-F1 maximum, the c1Mcs arm tests repeated exposure, and structure probing provides a task-specificity control. |
| **Unique-corpus size and epoch coverage covary.** Smaller-corpus arms can receive more epochs at the same nucleotide budget, so corpus quantity, repetition, and training duration are not interchangeable variables. | A corpus-size ranking cannot be interpreted as a pure unique-data effect across unmatched coverage. | Saturation comparisons are restricted to matched exposure/coverage, the c1Mcs repeated-exposure arm is used to test the duration account, and epoch counts accompany corpus-axis interpretations rather than being hidden by iso-token labels. |
| **The corpus is rRNA-biased.** A dominant rRNA channel can shape both absolute family scores and the layer at which non-rRNA family signal is easiest to read. | Full-corpus scores need not represent non-rRNA transfer, and deep-layer relocation cannot be interpreted without corpus stratification. | De-rRNA stratified reanalysis preserves the headline scale, mean-level attrition, and layer-shape directions, although absolute scores decrease. The P1 de-rRNA intervention removes the 650M shallow endpoint, directly testing the corpus-channel component while leaving the broader post-hoc quantitative account unconfirmed. |
| **Mean-pool results support only within-protocol, relative conclusions.** Pooling and head choice are readout interventions, not neutral implementations. | Absolute scores and baseline margins cannot be transferred between mean-pool and balanced protocols. | Both protocols are evaluated explicitly; for example, balanced s17 at 100M gives LM 0.1696 versus LightGBM 0.1760, whereas the adjudicated primary baseline table uses the separate mean-pool caliber. No cross-protocol difference is interpreted as a training effect. |
| **The 650M extended-budget retest is unreviewed and uses a different probe protocol.** Its summary values lack designated raw JSONL ledger rows, and the retest changes probe seeds, sample count, and layer-search window. | The retest supplies no current 650M-over-300M claim, training-seed replication, readout-stability conclusion, or same-protocol budget effect. | The factorial headline retains only the original terminal-protocol +1.15 pp single-seed E1 effect. Retest summaries are demoted to a handover-record citation in Appendix A rather than described as pending consolidation. |
| **The original split file is unrecoverable, and reconstruction is not equivalent.** The original member-to-representative mapping, clustering parquet, executable binary, and complete run log are missing. Although sequence content is recovered, the reconstructed cluster topology differs and places former training sequences in family-validation and family-test, violating pretraining-held-out isolation. | P2.5 retest consolidation and P2.7 random-terminal evaluation are stopped and demoted; P2.4 length-controlled RNS binning is not performed and its historical direction is withdrawn. P3.1 multi-seed training is not triggered because training on the reconstructed split would be leaky and incomparable, while a new data version would require retraining every comparison arm. | The final split adjudication preserves the reconstruction assets for future acceptance only if the original member mapping is recovered. No surrogate split is substituted, no leakage-affected probe is treated as valid, and the stopped analyses are not reported as negative results. |

These limitations lead directly to the provenance appendix, which preserves superseded values and protocol differences without allowing them to compete with the adjudicated main results. They prevent claims of composition-independent transfer, controlled external-corpus attribution, training-seed-robust large-scale effects, universal per-seed attrition, finalized retest provenance, a 650M random-terminal response, or an adjudicated RNS-binning mechanism. The rfamcap structure checkpoint is adjudicated, and external COV and RNS×COV are included with their observational limits; the unrecoverable split is an explicit evidence gap rather than unfinished work presented as forthcoming.
