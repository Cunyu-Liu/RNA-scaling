# Appendix A. Provenance of superseded values and reporting calibers

This appendix records which historical numbers were replaced or demoted during P0 adjudication and why they must not be mixed with the current results. The decisive sources are the frozen contradiction audit at old-DRAFT lines L598–L622, the standard-deviation and 650M retest recalculation, and the rfamcap iso-budget correction. The central provenance observation is that most discrepancies arise from changed comparators, checkpoints, protocols, or degrees of freedom rather than from new training runs.

## A.1 Structural-readout audit at old DRAFT L598–L622

The old composition-floor vector `1M −0.047 / 10M −0.010 / 30M −0.000 / 100M +0.013 / 650M +0.021` (L606–L608) is **superseded** and must not be used as a current scale result. The 1M row used a weak training subset (35k tokens versus 1.0M for the other scales), and the earlier comparison mixed paired-class F1 with a macro-F1 floor; a separate first multi-dataset run was excluded because sequence and pair labels were shuffled with different seeds (old DRAFT L345–L352; P0.5/C1–C2). The corrected own-best trained-minus-randinit values are `1M +0.007 / 10M −0.001 / 30M −0.003 / 100M +0.004`, a comparator-specific linear-readout result rather than a cross-channel estimate of pairing knowledge.

The corrected 1M `+0.007` above must not be confused with the old **family-classification baseline** `+0.007` at 100M. The latter compared balanced-probe LM s17 (approximately 0.170) with k-mer logistic regression (0.163), whereas the strongest observed classical comparator was LightGBM s17 at 0.1760; under the same balanced s17 protocol, LM = 0.1696 and the contrast is −0.0064 (P0.3). That old family-baseline `+0.007` is therefore **demoted to a non-primary historical comparator**, while the structurally distinct own-best trained-minus-randinit `+0.007` remains valid only with its comparator named. The current primary mean-pool observed-baseline contrast at 100M is +0.1634 using the three-training-seed LM mean against LightGBM s17, not a three-seed-versus-three-seed comparison.

The old inference that paired knowledge was broadly absent is **withdrawn**, not preserved as an alternative current conclusion. Later forward covariation, Partner-Oracle, and matched-prior pair-probe evidence establish pretraining-derived pairing signal while retaining the narrower finding that linear readout exposes only a small net increment at the tested scales (P0.5/C1). The numerical history is retained here for traceability without reproducing the withdrawn wording.

## A.2 Rfamcap checkpoint and budget caliber

The rfamcap 100M midpoint value `0.3448@L18` at 0.2B nt and its `+1.85 pp` contrast against main-line 100M `0.3263@L21` are **demoted to trajectory observations**. The comparator was a 1.9B terminal checkpoint, so the difference mixed corpus composition with budget; the main arms also lack matched 0.2B checkpoints, preventing a clean midpoint comparison (P0/B1). The adjudicated iso-budget terminal values are rfamcap `0.3297@L13` versus main `0.3263@L21`, giving `+0.34 pp` at 100M; at 30M, the terminal rfamcap value is `0.2880@L8`, giving `+4.14 pp` against `0.2466@L8`.

The historical rfamcap 100M structure value `0.5924@L20`, and hence its `+0.34 pp` difference from main `0.5890@L22`, is **withdrawn**, not merely checkpoint-unresolved. Neither the 1.8B nor the 1.9B candidate checkpoint reproduced any historical layer (0/23 exact-layer matches for each), and the historical source checkpoint remains unknown. The current 1.9B terminal contrasts instead use paired-F1: at 30M, rfamcap `0.5933@L11` versus main `0.5756@L3` gives `+1.77 pp`; at 100M, rfamcap `0.5904@L22` versus main `0.5890@L22` gives `+0.14 pp`. These new values supersede the historical structure effect and are reported separately from true macro-F1.

## A.3 Standard-deviation conventions

The population-standard-deviation versions `0.0107 / 0.0141 / 0.0132 / 0.0120` at 1M/10M/30M/100M (ddof=0) are **replaced in main-text summaries** by the sample-standard-deviation values `0.0131 / 0.0173 / 0.0162 / 0.0147` (ddof=1). Both versions use the same 12 training-seed best-layer F1 values and final checkpoints; their ratio is exactly \(\sqrt{3/2}\), so the discrepancy is purely the denominator convention (P0.2a). The ddof=0 values remain reproducible alternative summaries, not independent uncertainty measurements and not standard errors.

## A.4 The 650M extended-budget protocols

Under the original terminal protocol (20,000/4,000 probe sequences, full-layer scans, probe seed 17), 650M at 5.9B scores `0.3747@L25`, below 300M at `0.3821@L22` by `−0.74 pp`. This original result is **valid but protocol-specific**, not superseded: it remains the factorial input and yields the same-protocol 650M budget effect `0.3747 − 0.3632 = +1.15 pp` (P0.2b). Duplicate ledger rows were deduplicated and are not additional seeds.

The deep-window retest instead uses 8,000/4,000 sequences and probe seeds 29/43/61 on the same s17 trained weights, giving 650M `0.4491 ± 0.0147` versus 300M `0.3826 ± 0.0159`, a `+6.65 pp` mean paired difference with three of three probe-seed pairs positive. These values are **demoted to an unreviewed handover-record citation** and are not current evidence for readout stability, a 650M-over-300M advantage, training-seed replication, or sequence-bootstrap uncertainty. All 30 seed–layer values lack corresponding rows in the designated raw JSONL ledger; P2.5 consolidation is stopped because the original split cannot be recovered and the leaky reconstruction cannot support an equivalent retest.

The descriptive difference `0.4491 − 0.3632 = +8.59 pp` is **invalid as a budget-effect headline** because it crosses probe protocols, sample counts, layer windows, and probe-seed rules. Only `+1.15 pp` enters the original-terminal factorial table; a retest-based budget effect would require applying the same retest protocol to the 2.0B weights. The earlier `+6.5 pp` rendering of the 650M-minus-300M retest difference is also replaced by the correctly rounded `+6.65 pp` (or `+6.6 pp` to one decimal).

The 650M random-split value `−1.85 pp` is a **demoted historical 5B midpoint observation** (`0.6067` at 5B minus the 2B terminal value `0.6252`), not a terminal budget effect. P2.7 is stopped because the original split is unrecoverable and the reconstruction leaks former training sequences into evaluation partitions. The random-channel budget-insensitivity claim is therefore restricted to the three completed terminal comparisons; the open point in Figure 5 is a visual provenance marker for this appendix observation, not a fourth terminal result.

## A.5 Withdrawn RNS sequence-binning analysis

The historical 100M low/middle/high RNS-bin paired-position F1 values `0.4005/0.5011/0.4727` and long-range F1 values `0.5735/0.7430/0.7414`, together with the high-versus-low increases `+18.0%` and `+29.3%`, are **withdrawn to historical reference only**. The pair labels used one-based indices incorrectly and the probe heads were not seeded. These values must not support a sequence-binning direction, a reversal relative to proteins, or an axis-dependent mechanism. The historical length–RNS Spearman value `+0.1915` is likewise insufficient to adjudicate the proposed length-confound account. P2.4 length-controlled reanalysis is not performed under the final split adjudication. The RNS scale, training-time, cross-model, and family axes and the separate direct RNS–COV analysis are unaffected.

## A.6 Final split adjudication

The original split cannot be exactly restored because its member-to-representative mapping, original clustering parquet, executable binary, and complete run log are unavailable. Reconstruction recovers sequence content but changes cluster topology and introduces observable train-to-family-validation/test leakage, so it cannot be used to probe old checkpoints as though pretraining-held-out isolation still held. Accordingly, P2.5 and P2.7 are stopped and demoted, P2.4 is not performed and its binning claim is withdrawn, and P3.1 multi-seed supplementation is not triggered. A future new data version would require fresh preregistration and retraining of all comparison arms; these stopped analyses are neither completed nor negative results.

## A.8 Probe-convergence caliber (P2.10)

The earlier six-epoch structural-probe high scores `0.5973` (probe_structure) and `0.6101` (readout_heads linear) are **demoted to an unconverged caliber containing fitting artifacts**. Under the convergence discipline established by P2.10, linear heads must be trained to convergence before comparison; the converged per-token paired-position F1 at 650M resid_post is `0.5562`, which is the authoritative caliber. The unconverged values are retained for traceability only and must not be compared against converged heads or used as evidence of a higher structural ceiling. Structural readout uses non-saturated position F1 with matched-prior subtraction.

## A.9 Family budget single-seed magnitudes (P2.11)

The original single-seed factorial family budget effects `−5.24 / −2.74 / +3.76 / +1.15 pp` at 30M/100M/300M/650M are **archived as single-seed approximations**, not precise headlines: family F1 is sensitive to the data subsample (SD ~0.07), so magnitudes of ±1–4 pp lie within measurement noise. The P2.11/P2.12 multi-seed remeasurement on the recovered split (≈99.7% of original labels; contamination raises absolute F1 by ~0.06) gives paired budget effects of `−5.86 ± 0.91 pp` (0/10 positive) at 30M and `−1.65 ± 1.42 pp` (1/10 positive) at 100M — sign robustly negative — `−0.74 ± 4.38 pp` (5/10 positive) at 300M — uncertain, with the original `+3.76 pp` not reproduced — and `+3.06 ± 2.68 pp` (SEM 0.85, 9/10 positive) at 650M — sign robustly positive. These recovered-pool values are a near-original robustness probe, **not an exact reproduction** of the original factorial, and are not back-filled as such; exact magnitudes require the original member-to-cluster mapping. The cross-scale sign tendency (small-scale overtraining negative / large-scale undertraining positive) is the robust claim, now multi-seed supported at three of four scales; the 650M positive sign is not withdrawn.

## A.7 Status key

**Superseded** values are replaced because of a corrected metric, training subset, or statistical convention; **withdrawn** values fail reproduction or lose inferential validity and must not be used as current evidence; **demoted** values remain descriptive observations but lose causal or primary-table status because their comparators or checkpoints differ; **valid but protocol-specific** values remain current only inside their stated protocol; and **unreviewed handover-record** values are preserved for traceability without raw-ledger or split-file verification. These labels implement P0.5/C1–C2, P0/A1–A2/B1, and the final split adjudication without deleting negative or protocol-sensitive results.

This appendix closes the provenance trail for the limitations and prevents historical values from re-entering the Results as current evidence. It cannot repair the missing retest ledger rows, restore the original split, reproduce the withdrawn historical rfamcap structure value or identify its unknown source, create matched midpoints, validate the historical RNS-binning direction, or convert probe-seed repetition into independent training-seed replication.
